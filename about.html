The name

Each timber bears on the next

Ponte autoportante, after Leonardo da Vinci, Codex Atlanticus.

Leonardo drew a timber span that uses no nails, no dowels and no rope. Beams of like size are laid so each one bears on the next, and the whole is held by its own weight. Ogni legno regge l'altro: every timber holds up the other.

BridgeGap For Builders applies the same principle to the building trade. On every project, developers, contractors and subcontractors are the timbers of the span: each carries the others, and none stands alone. BridgeGap lays the footing they rest on, so the span stands on its own.
